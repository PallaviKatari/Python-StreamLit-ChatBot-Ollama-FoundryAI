Banking Customer Service Policy

1. Account Opening
Customers must provide valid identity and address proof
to open a savings account.

2. UPI Transactions
Customers should check their bank's official UPI transaction
limits because limits may differ by bank and account type.

3. Reporting Fraud
Customers should immediately report unauthorized transactions
through their bank's official fraud reporting channel.

4. Customer Support
Customers should use the official bank website or mobile
application to find customer support contact information.

5. Security
Customers must never share their PIN, OTP, password, or
complete card security details with anyone.
